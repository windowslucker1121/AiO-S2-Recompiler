# Code of conduct

This project is made by people in their free time. Everyone who takes part (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions) follows these
rules.

## Be respectful

- Be friendly and patient, also with people who are new to the project, to recompilation or to programming.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people. Explain why something is wrong and what would be better.
- No insults, harassment, threats, discriminating remarks, sexual content or personal attacks, and no publishing of other people's
  private information.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on a change, even when you disagree; you may ask for the reasons.

## Keep the project legal

- No piracy: do not ask for or share game files, dumps, keys, firmware or links to them, and no ways around copy protection.
- Do not post game material (game files, extracted data, saves, screenshots, videos or captures of a game) in issues or pull requests.
  The details are in [LEGAL.md](LEGAL.md).

## What happens when someone breaks these rules

Maintainers remove comments, issues or pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them. A warning
comes first unless the behaviour is severe (threats, harassment, illegal material).

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment, issue or pull request (the "..." menu), or contact a maintainer listed in
[`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS) through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately. Security problems go to the
Security tab ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

These rules apply to everything in this repository and to anyone who represents the project elsewhere.
